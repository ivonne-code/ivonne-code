## Highlights

**"SEO for the GPT era," built and demonstrated** — full-stack Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform showing how content changes move products up LLM-generated recommendation lists. Part of the iSchool research line behind *Controlling Output Rankings in Generative Engines for LLM-based Search* (CORE), which moves bottom-ranked items into the Top-1 **80.3%** of the time across four search-enabled LLMs.

**100k+ merchant records matched** — Jaro-Winkler fuzzy matching with geospatial constraints in R for the Tulsa RCT, surfacing measurable spending-distribution shifts between control and treatment groups; findings presented to faculty and city stakeholders.

**76% accurate Expected Goals (xG) model** — logistic regression and spatial features over **87,111 shots from 3,433 matches**, with heat maps of high-probability scoring zones.

**Deep-learning segmentation of mitochondrial networks** — U-Net in PyTorch with patch-based training to fit high-resolution microscopy into limited memory, turning morphology into quantifiable metrics for biology researchers.

**Regulatory review cycle cut by 5 days** — walk-through testing at CITIC Securities caught two disclosure gaps pre-submission; co-authored a 30-page industry brief adopted in road-show materials.

**200+ pages, 100% compliance, zero revision requests** — cross-document consistency checks and 40+ ABS / public REITs prospectus addenda aligned to the latest CSRC rules at Shenwan Hongyuan.
